Core spread = (q84 − q16)/2; shift = median(DATA) − median(MC), divided by MC spread. These are descriptive quantities, not fitted single-particle resolutions. Tails are measured outside MC's central 95% interval. Quantiles use finite, in-range histogram counts; underflow/overflow are reported separately. A narrow histogram, few entries, or coarse bins can invalidate the comparison.
