An active positivity constraint is not equivalent to an interior likelihood minimum. The large Minuit EDM near the hard invalid-likelihood penalty is not a reliable measure of physical error size. The direct analytic score and curvature above avoid that penalty. KKT residuals test whether the score can be balanced by nonnegative multipliers of the active positivity constraints. A small residual is evidence for a constrained boundary solution, not an unconstrained Gaussian fit.

Boundary-distance diagnostics are local linear Gaussian checks, not confidence intervals. Reaching the boundary within two covariance standard deviations flags potentially misleading symmetric errors even for an optimizer-valid fit.

A spin-conditional likelihood has no UU information when every spin-dependent amplitude is zero. Small spin signals can therefore give weak or nonregular UU estimates. This audit does not add external constraints or fix any amplitudes.
