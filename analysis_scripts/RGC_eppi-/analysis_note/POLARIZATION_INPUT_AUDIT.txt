4. What the default pi+ processing does
processing_two_particles.groovy defaults p1 PID to 211 and uses TwoParticles: detected electron plus pion; the neutron is inferred through missing mass, not required as a detected particle.
The Groovy script outputs kinematics and helicity. It does NOT choose target polarization. convert_txt_to_root.cpp script_index=1,is_mc=0 loads analysis_scripts/asymmetry_extraction/imports/clas12_run_info.csv and looks up target_polarization by run. For run<16000 it sets target_pol=0.
The dedicated eppi- campaign inherited the same signed ND3 metadata through its saved CSV. No new target-sign convention was introduced in this note.

5. What the pi+ analysis uses
RGC_enpi+/asymmetry_extraction/extract_structure_function_ratios.py defaults to its analysis-local clas12_run_info.csv. Its parser explicitly selects NH3 rows for the nominal pi+ analysis, takes Pt from column 5, Q+ and Q- from columns 3 and 4, and retains fully zero-charge runs only for bookkeeping.
The optional DIS diagnostic loads PbPt_NH3_summer22_F1F221_2025-06-24.xlsx, PbPt_NH3_fall22_F1F221_2025-06-24.xlsx, PbPt_NH3_spring23_F1F221_2025-06-24.xlsx. These are NH3 inputs, NOT the attached ND3 PDF_SF_2025-07-15 workbooks. They do not replace the nominal CSV by default.
The pi+ DIS diagnostic passes the signed workbook PbPt products into its alternate LL fit. The reference note also has absolute-polarization comparison plots; an absolute comparison is distinct from a signed likelihood replacement.
