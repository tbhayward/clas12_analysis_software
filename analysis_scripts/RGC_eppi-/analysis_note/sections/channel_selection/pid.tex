\subsection{Particle Identification}\label{sec::pid}
The scattered electron is identified in the FD using the vertex, calorimeter sampling-fraction, diagonal and fiducial requirements of the established RGC electron selection, described in the \href{https://clas12-docdb.jlab.org/DocDB/0012/001255/001/RGC_FD_Electron_Particle_Identification.pdf}{RGC electron PID note}. The campaign requires electron momentum above 2 GeV.
Negative pions are EventBuilder PID $-211$ candidates in the FD satisfying $-3<\mathrm{chi2pid}<3$. CD pions are excluded. Protons are EventBuilder PID 2212 candidates in the FD or CD. No additional explicit proton chi2pid cut is imposed in this campaign; values are saved for inspection. Proton momentum thresholds are 0.30 GeV in the CD, 0.50 GeV in FD inbending and 0.42 GeV in FD outbending. The proton polar angle is below $64.23^\circ$, and the established vertex and detector-specific fiducial cuts are retained. There is no upper proton-momentum cut. Proton energy-loss corrections are applied. Exclusivity is expected to improve purity, but residual misidentification must still be checked rather than assumed negligible.
