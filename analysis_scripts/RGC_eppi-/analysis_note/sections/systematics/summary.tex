\subsection{Summary of Systematic Uncertainties}\label{sec:systematics}
Point-to-point uncertainties are evaluated separately for each kinematic bin and observable. The inherited bookkeeping is
\begin{equation}\delta_{ptp}=\sqrt{\delta_{rad}^2+\delta_{ch}^2+\delta_{migration}^2}.\end{equation}
The numerical point-to-point table is intentionally blank. Radiation and migration are preliminary $\pi^+$ transfers, not new $\pi^-$ determinations. Channel selection is to be measured here. No conclusion that misidentification or nuclear effects are negligible is imported from the proton analysis. Bound/free-neutron and target-axis interpretations remain distinct from demonstrated experimental uncertainties until the relevant studies are complete.
